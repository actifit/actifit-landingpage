@everyone

🏟️ **The Arena is live.**

Six fitness challenges are open right now — and for the first time, **you can create your own** and invite the whole community to it.

**What's live today**
🥇 Weekly Step League — climb the weekly leaderboard
🎯 Daily Focus Goal — hit your goal, keep the streak
🪜 Season Ladder — a two-week climb
🌍 Weekly Global Top-N — compete with everyone
⚡ Weekend Warrior — 48-hour blitz
🗓️ Monthly Live-Ops — a month of milestones

They recur, so if you miss one window the next comes around.

**Three things are true of every challenge**
🆓 Free to enter — no fee, no stake, no buy-in, ever
✅ Decided by your own verified Actifit activity
🚫 Never pay-to-win — you can't buy a better placing

**Make your own**
Head to <https://actifit.io/arena/create>. Name a collectible **badge** and pick who earns it (winner / top 3 / everyone) — free, open to all. Or **fund a real AFIT prize** from your balance; winners split it 50/30/20.

Everything is signed with **your own Hive posting key** and recorded on-chain, so results are public and verifiable — not a number we could quietly change.

Available in all **14 languages** from day one. 🌐
