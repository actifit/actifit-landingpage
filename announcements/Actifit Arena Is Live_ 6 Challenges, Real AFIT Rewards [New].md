Hey Actifitters! 💪

**The Actifit Arena is live.** Six recurring fitness challenges are open right now, they reward real AFIT for verified activity, every finisher can collect badges — and for the first time, **any Actifitter can create their own challenge** and invite the whole community to it.

This is one of the biggest additions to Actifit in years, because until now your daily activity earned you rewards on your own. The Arena lets you put it up against everyone else's — on a leaderboard, in a duel, or in a challenge you design yourself. 👇

![Actifit Arena launch banner — fitness challenges, leaderboards and AFIT rewards](https://usermedia.actifit.io/MUFFCET52PJIHUSWD0B5OD37T61EEP)

---

## 🏟️ What Is The Arena?

The Arena is a new home for competition on Actifit, live at **[actifit.io/arena](https://actifit.io/arena)**.

Instead of only tracking your own steps, you can now enter challenges that score everyone on the same rules and settle automatically when the window closes, for example a weekly step league that ranks every entrant on verified activity. Every challenge shows you its scoring metric, its window, its reward, and a live leaderboard while it runs.

Three things are true of **every** challenge in the Arena:

* **🆓 Free to enter.** There is no entry fee, no stake, no buy-in — ever. Joining a challenge never costs you anything.
* **✅ Decided by verified activity.** Your score comes from your own verified Actifit activity reports. No voting, no luck, no chance.
* **🚫 Never pay-to-win.** You cannot buy a better placing, and nobody wagers anything against anybody. This is a skill-and-effort competition, by design.

![The Actifit Arena — six live fitness challenges with leaderboards and AFIT rewards](https://usermedia.actifit.io/MUFFD8WGBI0F934ZDCHODJ5E6MTW)

## 🎯 The Six Launch Challenges

The Arena opens with six official contests, and they recur — so if you miss one window, the next one comes around:

* **🥇 Weekly Step League** — climb the weekly leaderboard by staying active every day. *Recurs weekly.*
* **🎯 Daily Focus Goal** — hit your daily step goal and keep your streak alive. *Recurs daily.*
* **🪜 Season Ladder** — a two-week climb to the top of the ladder. *Seasonal.*
* **🌍 Weekly Global Top-N** — compete with the whole community to finish in the global Top-N. *Recurs weekly.*
* **⚡ Weekend Warrior** — a 48-hour weekend blitz. Go all out. *Every weekend.*
* **🗓️ Monthly Live-Ops Event** — a month-long event with milestones all the way to the finish. *Recurs monthly.*

Official contests are funded by Actifit and therefore reward **AFIT** — the same token you already earn, spend in the market, and can cash out. To keep emissions healthy and fair, official contest rewards are capped at **500 AFIT per person per day**.

Each challenge has its own page showing the rules, the window, the reward and a live leaderboard:

![A live Actifit Arena challenge page — Weekly Step League, showing rules, window, AFIT prize and standings](https://usermedia.actifit.io/MUFFE185MA1MBU8P4WGJVB3CECZCUP)

## 🛠️ Create Your Own Challenge

This is the part we're most excited about. Head to **[actifit.io/arena/create](https://actifit.io/arena/create)** and a short wizard walks you through it: name it, pick the type, set the window, choose how it's scored, decide who can join — and pick the reward.

You get two ways to reward your players:

* **🏅 Badge only — free, open to everyone.** Name a collectible badge, then choose **who earns it**: the winner only, the top 3 finishers, or everyone who completes the challenge. Costs you nothing to run.
* **🪙 Fund an AFIT prize.** Put up a real AFIT prize from your own balance. Winners split it **50 / 30 / 20**, a **5% platform fee** applies, and funding a prize requires holding at least **20,000 AFIT**. As the funder you **cannot win your own prize**, and any unpaid remainder comes back to you when the challenge settles.

You can make a challenge **public**, or keep it **private** and share it by link — handy for a squad, a gym, or a friendly grudge match. 😄

---

## 🎖️ Badges You Actually Keep

When a challenge settles, earned badges are recorded against your result — and they show up in two places: on the challenge page next to the finishers who earned them, and in a new **Arena Challenge Badges** section on your Actifit profile.

Badges are only awarded to people who actually put in verified activity, because otherwise anyone could spin up a challenge and hand themselves a trophy for doing nothing. And because anyone can create a challenge, badges from community-made challenges carry a small **"Community"** marker — so an official Actifit badge is always distinguishable from one a user minted themselves.

## ⛓️ Built On Hive, Signed By You

The Arena is **chain-first**. When you join, leave, or create a challenge, your client broadcasts a signed `actifit_arena` operation to the Hive blockchain using **your own posting key**. Actifit's backend then indexes what the chain says.

That means the record of who entered, who competed, and how a challenge settled is public, verifiable Hive history — not a number in a private database we could quietly change. Results are signed, and they're auditable by anyone. 🔍

## 🚀 How To Get Started

1. Open **[actifit.io/arena](https://actifit.io/arena)** (or tap **The Arena** 🏆 in the top menu).

You will also find it right on the Actifit home page:

![The Arena promo on the Actifit home page, linking to browse or create a challenge](https://usermedia.actifit.io/MUFFKI7BV22P382ZCYHYL4W9J66DVA)

2. Pick a challenge that fits how you already move, and hit **Join** — it's free.
3. Keep posting your activity reports as usual. Your score updates automatically while the challenge runs.
4. When the window closes, rewards and badges are settled and credited for you.

That's it. No entry fee, no extra app, no separate wallet. If you're already tracking your activity with Actifit, you're already qualified. 🏃‍♀️🏃
